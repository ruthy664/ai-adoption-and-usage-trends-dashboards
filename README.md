# AI Adoption and Usage Trends
**Dataset:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/eishatuzzuhra/ai-usage-and-impact-on-students-and-professionals

**Intended Audience:** Business professionals, researchers, and anyone interested in AI usage and adoption trends.
## Project Overview
This Project includes three dashboards exploring *AI Usage and Impact on Students & Professionals* dataset from kaggle, comparing different AI models, individuals who use them, money spent on these models, and comparing how different countries adopt this technology.

The dataset includes 289 responses and 19 features, including information about the AI tools, the individuals surveyed, and their experiences using AI.
## Dashboard 1: Comparison and Demographics
The first dashboard compares eight AI models across several dimensions, including the number of users, popular use cases, money spent monthly on AI, and more.

The dashboard also includes demographic information about gender and students vs professionals, allowing viewers to filter by these demographics as well as different AI models.

## Dashboard 2: Money Spent
The second dashboard, Money Spent, explores how much money users spend monthly on AI and how different factors may play into it, including time saved, user satisfaction, and the hours users spend per day using AI.

This dashboard includes charts showing how much money is spent based on the primary purpose of AI (such as content creation and writing), as well as how much time and money users spend based on their education level.

## Dashboard 3: Countries
The final dashboard, Countries, compares AI use and adoption across 13 countries, with the majority of participants coming from India, Canada, and the US.

This dashboard explores how many individuals would recommend AI from each country, the most common uses for AI in each country, and how much money is spent monthly on average from each country.
